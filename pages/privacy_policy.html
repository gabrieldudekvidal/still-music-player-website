Still Music Player Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Thank you for using Still Music Player. Still Music Player is a local music player for macOS. Your privacy is a core part of the app's design. The app does not require an account, does not upload your music to the cloud, and does not collect your personal information.

Information We Collect

Still Music Player does not collect any personal information.

The app does not require you to:

  • Create an account
  • Sign in
  • Provide your name, email address, or any other personal information

Your Music

Your music files remain on your Mac at all times. Still Music Player only accesses the music folders and files that you choose. The app uses this access to display your music library and play your local music files.

Your music is never:

  • Uploaded to the cloud
  • Sent to the developer
  • Shared with third parties
  • Used for advertising, analytics, or machine learning

Local Data

The app may store local preferences, such as your selected music library folder and application settings. This information is stored only on your Mac and is never transmitted elsewhere.

Internet Connection

Still Music Player is designed to work offline. An internet connection is not required to browse your music library or play your local music files.

Analytics and Tracking

Still Music Player does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • User profiling

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with third-party services that collect or process your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Still Music Player does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date at the top of this document.
